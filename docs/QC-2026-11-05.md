# Studio portfolio revision — 5 November 2026

Scope: the client-selected clothesline/studio display gallery, About revision, original application logos, plain LinkedIn contact and the additional Mantra Talks reel. The existing palette and typography are retained.

## Implemented

- All eleven projects use landscape 3:2 display cards, a fine hanging line, small clothespins and a slight paper tilt. Previous/next buttons, native horizontal scrolling and keyboard controls replace the previous grid. The gallery does not autoplay.
- Logo covers are preferred. The shared Drive was checked again and four new files were downloaded for Tutorising, Sportlight, HUMAS and PPI Malaysia. These complement the four existing project logos. Digital Print Production, Alice and Photography retain artwork/photo covers.
- The student bio appears once, in a pale-yellow speech bubble beside the mascot on the right. The old bio beneath the greeting is removed. What I do uses editorial rules, unframed icons and readable service names instead of circular badges.
- Seven application icons replace the proficiency rings and letter placeholders. Application colors and shapes are preserved. Vendor source URLs and local hashes are recorded in SOFTWARE_LOGO_SOURCES.json. The icons also identify software on project detail pages.
- LinkedIn matches the other contact rows: its icon, label and the name Darren Ryusa. The name remains a normal profile link, without a boxed button. No email or phone controls were added.
- Mantra Talks now has three local reel players. The additional source is https://www.instagram.com/reel/DL7ABI3vN9p/. Its original 360×640 resolution is retained when encoding, rather than upscaling. Native controls, playsinline, preload none and no autoplay remain.

## Asset provenance and limits

Original application files were obtained from Adobe, Canva and CapCut-owned sources. Adobe and Canva SVG files are copied unchanged; CapCut’s official favicon image is converted losslessly to PNG. No app icon was generated or redrawn. Four Drive logo downloads were checked against expected sizes and recorded with hashes before being optimized for the web. Raw originals remain in the ignored Asset directory; web copies are tracked.

Checks used Chromium on Windows and responsive viewport sizes. Actual touch hardware, Safari/macOS and OS phone/email applications were not exercised. Swipe uses native browser overflow and scroll snap; arrow buttons provide an alternative. No messages were sent and no external profile account was changed. No push or Vercel deployment is performed by this commit.

Earlier dated QC documents describe previous layouts. This report supersedes their grid, proficiency-ring, LinkedIn-button and two-reel presentation details.
